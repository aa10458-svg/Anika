Facts about Red Pandas

Red pandas are endangered, solitary mammals that are not closely related to giant pandas;
they are more closely related to raccoons and skunks
Red pandas are small, arboreal mammals from the Himalayas,
known for their reddish-brown fur, bushy ringed tails, and unique "pseudo-thumb"
for gripping bamboo, though they're related to raccoons, not giant pandas
